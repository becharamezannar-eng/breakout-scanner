How to read this

  • Look at AvgR (expectancy per trade, in units of risk) and ProfitFactor (gross wins / gross losses). Above 0 and above 1.0 respectively means an edge; the Gate-pass row should beat the Gate-fail row if the scorecard helps.
  • Small groups (under ~30 trades) are noise.
  • Limits: today's index members only (survivorship bias flatters results), no sector or earnings data in history (those score neutral), daily bars only (no intraday VWAP check), no commissions beyond slippage.
