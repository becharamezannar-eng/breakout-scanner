What the labels mean

  • Buy Breakout confirmed and it passes the scorecard (70+/100, reward:risk 2+, no earnings within 5 sessions, under 4 warnings). Entry plan below.
  • Watch Breakout not confirmed yet, or it held but doesn't pass the scorecard. Not a buy yet.
  • Don't chase Breakout held but price ran too far above the breakout level. Wait for a pullback.
  • Hold You own it (if you took the Buy). Keep the stop order in place.
  • Take profit Target hit. Sell part, protect the rest.
  • Hold - trailing Holding the remainder with a stop that rises with the price.
  • Sell Exit the position (or your stop order already did).
  • Drop Setup failed or never qualified before entry. Remove from watchlist.

Entry, stop and target are calculated from the breakout level. Profit/loss is measured from the close on the day of the Buy signal; your real entry is the next open. A Buy is exited early if it closes back below the breakout level within 10 sessions (failed breakout).

The score is a pass mark, not a ranking: in the 2022-26 backtest, setups scoring 70-74 did as well as or better than those scoring 75+. Position size risks 1% of the account per trade, so a run of 20 losses (Douglas's sample size) would cost about 18%.
